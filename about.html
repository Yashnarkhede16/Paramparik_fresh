Born from Tradition

✦ ✦ ✦

Paramparik Collection was founded by Neha Borole, a passionate artisan from Maharashtra with a deep love for India's rich jewellery heritage. Growing up surrounded by the vibrant colours and intricate craftsmanship of traditional Indian ornaments, Neha always dreamed of preserving and celebrating this art form.

What began as a personal passion soon grew into a mission — to bring authentic, handcrafted Indian jewellery to women who cherish their roots. Every Nath, Bindi, Bangle, and Earring in the Paramparik Collection is lovingly made by hand, using techniques passed down through generations.

Neha believes that jewellery is more than an accessory — it is an emotion, a memory, and a celebration of identity. Each piece is crafted with patience, precision, and an unwavering commitment to quality, ensuring that every woman who wears it feels connected to something timeless.

“Jewellery is not just an accessory — it is an emotion.”

— Neha Borole
